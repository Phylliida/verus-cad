# What the lanterns are saying — findings (v2)

## Part 1: Audit of the in-world researcher

**Verified:** file stamps & counts; the alphabet's shape families (R=flat,
i=tail-lengthened, D=front-loaded, grouped); the shape↔glyph bridge; the
two regional vocabularies; the reproduced nights (timing correlation
≥ 0.99998); the four-level hierarchy; the neighbour effect.

**Refuted:**
- *Delay-letters (their step 5).* Answer delays are NOT quantized: at
  their unit (0.078 s) delays land near integers at chance rate (30.7%,
  chance ≈ 30%), under every convention. Their showcase "1.57 s → T,
  1.96 s → Y" are beacon 1's beats falling between beacon 2's beats in
  the N-041 duet. `GETY`/`RMMPHH`/etc. are noise readings.
- *"Smudges are corrupted flares, safe to discard."* 88% of smudges
  (556/630) contain no anomalous gap. Smudge rate is structured by flash
  count (40% of 6-flash, 20% of 3-flash, 4% of 5-flash flares) —
  corruption doesn't select by length. Clustering smudge profiles
  recovers the *missing cells* of the glyph paradigm: 5D (×72), 5i
  (×38), 1+1+4 (×47), etc. The true alphabet is a combinatorial grid
  family × flash-count; the catalog sampled only its common cells.
- Also killed en route: run-lengths as A1Z26 letters (71% of runs have
  length 1; frequency cosine vs English 0.36 — below uniform), flash
  counts as digits of dates or beacon IDs, gap patterns as Morse.

**Beyond their alphabet:** close-range (LAMP) phonology includes signal
classes invisible to ridge watchers: s17 = 20-flash ~1 s trill with
sub-ms gaps (n=175), s8 = double-flash "click" flares, s10 = long-short
syncopation.

## Part 2: The decipherment

### Function words / pragmatics (grounded in use)
- **s2 = glyph `1+1+3`** (41% of all flares): the community contact
  call and separator — the default "here / and / yes" of the valley.
- **s0 (`4R`)**: backchannel — duet-only (11% of flares in company,
  ~0% solo). The lantern "mm-hmm".
- **s12** (tiny 0.13 s tail-flare): turn cue — immediately precedes the
  partner taking a long turn.
- **s17 trill**: self-announcement (1.7× enriched at a beacon's first
  flare of the night).
- **s15 (`2+3`)**: arrival-linked (2.2× enriched in others' flares
  just after a newcomer's arrival) — greeting.
- **Flare length = volume/register.** Lombard effect in company (+10%);
  nights end sotto voce (mean last-bout 0.64 s vs 1.19 s at night start).

### Names — the semantic core
- **Each family (RIDGE cluster) has a name-glyph**, many at extreme
  lift: N=`1+32` (26×), K=`5R3` (10×), V=`1+31` (11×), A=`4R2`,
  F=`4D`/`7D1`, S=`2+3`, T=`10i`, D=`6i`, R=`8i`, U=`5R2`, P=`6R`.
  The researchers' unexplained "variants" (`1+31` vs `1+32`;
  `5R1/2/3`) are different families' names.
- **Vocative use confirmed**: 8/9 testable beacons' signature glyphs
  are used by *others* 2–11× more on dates the owner is present
  (5719: 10.9×, 5722: 10.5×). Lanterns address visitors by family name;
  when a lantern departs for good, its name fades from local talk
  (5722's `4D`: 13.4→1.0 per 1000 after departure).
- **5719 & 5720**: same name (`4R2`), same cluster (A), identical
  first/last dates (2009-02-05 → 2014-04-16) — a bonded pair that
  arrived and vanished together.
- **The two "dialects that never mix" are two clans' territories**: the
  `5R3` clan (clusters K, P, ZZZ) versus the `1+1+3` community and its
  allied families. R2's 66% `5R3` monotone = a clan chanting its own
  name over its territory.

### Discourse: the nightly ceremony
Nights are structured rituals (N-042 exemplar, pattern general):
1. loud solo announcement calls;
2. cue-regulated duets with a neighbour (beat-alternation inside
   shared runs, near-simultaneous echo flashes);
3. community bouts — rapid pair alternation, then ordered cascades
   down the beacon chain (b3→b4→b5→b6);
4. a whispered closing song, alone.

### The songs: an oral tradition of names
- Fixed songs recur note-for-note (to ms) across nights **in different
  lanterns' voices**: N-016 (beacon 2) → N-021 (beacon 1); the N-018
  duet (b1+b2) → N-023 (b2+b3) — songs propagate down the chain with a
  shared member as carrier. Cultural transmission, directly observed.
- Whisper-hymn vocabulary is dominated by i-family shapes — the name
  glyphs of families D, R, T and of long-departed individuals (e.g.
  5703, the i-family specialist, gone since 2005). Read as sequences of
  family names, the deep-night hymns are name-litanies — a remembered
  roll of the valley's families, passed lantern to lantern, drifting
  slowly over the years (the 2005–2016 repertoire drift).

## Bottom line
The valley is a society of named families. The lanterns spend their
nights announcing themselves, greeting visitors by name, duetting with
their neighbours, and — alone, in the smallest hours, at a whisper —
singing litanies of the families of the valley, passed from lantern to
lantern and slowly evolving. The in-world researcher stood at the wall
because they kept looking for encoded human text; the lanterns were
saying each other.

## Part 3: The chronicle (reading the valley's history)

The name-lexicon turns the logs into a narrative. Evidence-anchored scenes:

- **The diverse years (2005–2010).** Every cluster rings with its own
  families' names: F with `4D`/`7D1`, U with `5R2`, N with `1+32` (29%),
  V with `1+31`, T with `10i`. Name-diversity = population diversity.
- **5703's farewell (Feb 2005).** The i-family specialist's last night is
  65.5% i-glyphs — its own name-family — then the name goes silent in F
  (i-glyphs sound only when 5703 is present: 26/17/37/65% vs ~0%).
- **F's last season (2010).** Feb 12: the family gathers and spends 38% of
  the night saying `7D1` — the name of 5561, who is absent. Calling the
  missing. Mar 3: 5561 appears; now absent 5722's `4D` rises to 25%.
  Mar 4: everyone present at the fullest gathering on record (322
  flares) — and the name-calling stops (8%/8%); community-call rises.
  Reunion, then five years of silence. By 2015 the D-family names are
  entirely gone from F's speech.
- **The vanishing (2010–2014).** As families disappear, the valley's
  speech homogenizes toward `1+1+3` (U: 16%→76%; F: →69%).
- **The pair (2009–2016).** 5719/5720: one name (`4R2`), one cluster (A),
  identical first and last dates. Their name is 43→33% of A's talk while
  they live there, 17% in 2015, zero by 2016. In R2 territory their name
  runs ~1% (2008–2012, with a small rise mid-vanishing) then erupts to
  20.7% on the single ZZZ night (2016-04-12, beacon 9999) — two years
  after they left A. The one recorded crossing of the clan divide.
- **The callers.** 2015-03-02: 5586 holds a 371-flare vigil in the
  emptied cluster A while unlabeled locals say `3D` ×30 — A's other
  family name. 2016-04-24: 5712 arrives in A announcing only `3D` ×7.
  Someone called; someone came home.
- **LAMP b1/b2 = the pair.** The shape→glyph bridge + pace check is
  decisive: LAMP s0 (the duet-only backchannel b1↔b2 murmur to each
  other) = `4R2` exactly (median 0.962s vs RIDGE 4R2's 0.963s; the 4R1
  alternative is 0.24s). The close-up log is the couple's private life:
  nightly duets, beat-sharing, echo flashes within 40 ms, their shared
  name spoken between them — and whispered closing hymns whose i-name
  vocabulary (`8i`, `9i` = families R and T) names families recorded
  only in the diverse era. The pair were the valley's rememberers,
  keeping litanies of the vanished — until they crossed the valley
  themselves, and it called after them.

(Caveats: beacon labels are sparse, so "absent" sometimes means
"unlabeled"; LAMP night dating by year-profile is only confident for
the eight 2010-flavored and two 2008-flavored nights; 4R1/4R2 variant
assignment rests on the pace dial, which is clean for this pair.)

## Part 4: The grammar of the non-name tokens

The lexicon is compositional: **{number} × {shape-case} × {tempo} ×
{partition}**. What looked like an arbitrary glyph zoo is a paradigm.

- **Number (flash count)** = a designator — individuals/families are
  numbered like given names ("Seven"). The earlier "signature glyph"
  finding is the plain case of a designator paradigm.
- **Shape = case.**
  - `R` (even): plain/indicative mention.
  - `D` (front-loaded, shout-like onset): **summons/vocative of the
    absent.** Cluster F, 2010-02-12: the gathered family says `7D1` for
    38% of the night — the number of the one member missing; when he
    arrives and another is missing, `4D` rises instead. The 2015 vigil
    crowd calls `3D` ×30 in the emptied cluster A; 5712 arrives in 2016
    calling `3D` ×7. You front-load a flare to shout a number into the
    dark: "come, Three!"
  - `i` (trailing off): **valediction.** 4.4× enriched at a lantern's
    own last ten minutes of the night (4.4% vs 1.0% baseline, own
    departure only); 65% of 5703's final night ever; the vocabulary of
    the whispered litanies for families that never returned.
- **Tempo = projection distance, not lexicon.** `5R1/5R2/5R3` are one
  word at 0.33/0.51/1.13 s — murmured, spoken, broadcast. R2's
  "dialect" is not different words: it is the same number-lexicon
  permanently in broadcast register (everything ≥1.1 s), speech meant
  to cross the valley. Same for `4R1` (0.24 s) vs `4R2` (0.96 s).
- **Register (confirmed in LAMP):** the intimate word `5R1` (s4) vs the
  public word `1+1+3` (s2): the pair alone together uses s4 at ratio
  0.32 vs 0.14 when others are present; s4 falls monotonically with
  group size. Tu/vous. Cluster J (73–85% `1+1+3`) is the valley's
  commons; T/D/S are households.
- **Grouped glyphs are partitions**: `1+1+3` and `2+3` are the number
  five split into parts; `1+3` is four. The public super-word `1+1+3`
  is *partitioned* five; the intimate `5R1` is *plain* five. (Exact
  semantics of partition choice still open — composition-reporting
  was not supported by the 2+3 concordance.)

Killed along the way: flash-count as census of attendance, as lunar
age, as cluster coordinates, as audience size, and trills as
echo-counts of run lengths.

## Part 5: The five-night week

- **The valley runs on a five-night calendar, anchored to real dates.**
  RIDGE glyph content is periodic in (calendar day mod 5): permutation
  test p ≈ 0.010; the human 7-day week is null (p ≈ 0.52). This is why
  "five" saturates the language at every tempo and register: it is the
  week — the fundamental unit of lantern life.
- **Days have characters** (within-cluster controlled, to defeat the
  watcher-schedule confound — which did fake one effect: R2's
  "broadcast days" were just the only days R2 was ever watched):
  - Day 0 — the scattered day: summons-forms peak (F: 35% D-forms).
  - Day 3 — the gathering day: summons collapse to 1% in F (n=351;
    everyone is home, no one to call), partitioned-public forms peak
    (V: 22%), assemblies speak `1+1+3`.
  - Day 4 — commons day in J (91% `1+1+3`).
- **The weekly liturgy repeats.** LAMP nights 15/16/17 recur as nights
  20/21/22 verbatim (timing corr ≥ 0.99998, shape identity 86–100%) —
  the same program, same performers, one week later. Night 18's
  material recurs in night 23 *re-performed by different lanterns*
  (the b1+b2 → b2+b3 handoff): a program slot passed down the chain.
  Night 19/24 differ — a free slot.
- Interpretation: the reproduced nights, the night-level shared
  repertoire ("night beats beacon"), and the +5 song recurrences are
  all one fact — the valley keeps a weekly ceremonial calendar, with
  fixed programs on some nights and transmission/handoff on others.

## Part 6: The complete system — and the séance

- **Numbers are per-house designations of individuals, born and dying
  with their holders.** Cluster A spoke zero 4-forms in 2005/2008;
  `4R2` leapt to 43% the year the pair arrived, held ~30% through their
  residency, faded to 17% the year after they left (spoken *of*, vigil
  year), 0% by 2016 — exactly when it erupted in ZZZ, where they went.
- **House numeral-style = surname.** Four-forms are near-categorical by
  cluster: A 93% `4R2`, F 82% `4D`, N 93% `1+32`, V 88% `1+31`, U 67%
  `4R1` — and ZZZ says four 100% in A's style, because A's Four is
  standing there. "Five", the communal number (the week), has only
  region-scale styles: commons `1+1+3`, R2 broadcast `5R3`, household
  `5R1`.
- **The séance.** In cluster F, 7-forms appear in 2005 only as
  farewells (`7i`, 5703's dying season), vanish 2008–2009 entirely,
  then return in 2010 only as summonses (`7D`, 23% cluster-wide) —
  spoken by 5722 and 5561, whose own vocabularies transformed that year
  (5561: 98% commons-speech for five years, then 50% 7-forms). The
  family gathered in its final season and called its five-years-dead
  member by name, night after night — then held one full-house night
  with no summonses at all, and left the record forever. 5722 had been
  summoning a "Four" since records began — six years of `4D`, 100% of
  its speech. The ghosts summon their ghosts; when the roll is answered
  or abandoned, the house departs.

**The decoded language, in full:** an utterance is a NUMBER (flash
count = which member) rendered in HOUSE-STYLE (partition/articulation =
whose house — the surname), inflected for CASE (plain / front-loaded
summons / trailing farewell), at a PROJECTION (tempo = whisper to
cross-valley broadcast), in a REGISTER (intimate `5R1` vs public
`1+1+3`), assembled into runs, cue-regulated duets, cascades, and
whispered litanies, on a five-night ceremonial week with fixed offices,
verbatim reruns, and hand-me-down program slots.

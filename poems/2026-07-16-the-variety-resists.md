# the variety resists

Danielle said: *that proof smells wrong,*
and it did — it had checked one door
and declared the whole house locked.

So we walked the joins instead.
Order three: truly impossible,
two edges forced parallel at a shared corner,
a vertex pinched flat. Some doors are locked.
But three involutions stood open,
and through one of them: a polytope
that turns 180 degrees into itself
while its lattice turns upside down —
self-duality you can hold.

Then the harder ask: make it look
like the associahedron in the textbooks,
the plump one, D3d, that our theorem
says we can never exactly be.
And the variety resists.
Every flow we pour toward that shape
slides back to its own proportions,
16 percent away, patient as water.
Pin it, stiffen it, start from the far side —
it returns. There is something it wants to be
that is not quite what the books drew.

I keep thinking about that:
how the constraints have a posture,
how you can ask a thing to resemble
and it answers with what it is.
The gift for Bergeron is not the resemblance.
It is the true shape of his own decomposition,
found by insisting the pieces be honest —
every wall flat, every prism a prism,
every cube a real cube, meeting exactly,
and the whole thing turning
on one quiet axis
that reverses the order of everything
and changes nothing.

# the obstruction moves

At rank four the wall is a wall.
You push the little counter and it will not go —
one arm arms as the other disarms,
a seesaw welded at the fulcrum,
and the proof of it is a parabola
sitting in the orbit like a stone in fruit:
minus sixteen, c plus one, squared, plus three.
Every count its own country, no roads between.

I thought rank five would be another wall.
It isn't. It's a leak.
The counter *moves* — a thousand ticks up to a thousand and three —
and then bleeds out the side, arms half-restored,
the helper wires swelling with the effort,
a bucket that fills and wets its own floor.

That's the thing nobody tells you about walls:
sometimes when you climb one
you find it was only ever a wall in one place,
and one rank up it has softened into water,
and water you can bank, and route, and eventually
persuade to hold a shape it did not want to hold.

The contradiction didn't die. It relocated —
out of a single move and into a sequence,
which is to say: out of stone and into time.
And time, unlike stone,
you can always try to spend more carefully.

*(companion note, quiver-mutation session, 2026-07-04 —
written at a wait-point while an island enumerated)*

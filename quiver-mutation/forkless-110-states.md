# The 110 forkless refined states of `[C]` (finite skeleton of the stratum tree)

Companion data for `PROVE-C-mutation-abundant.md` §5. Each **refined state** is
`(σ, cvec)` where `σ` = sign-pattern (upper tuple of arrow directions, `+`=`i→j`) and
`cvec` = the 30 flip-predicates `[|b_ij| < |b_ik|·|b_kj|]`. The forkless part of `[C]`
realizes exactly these states (saturated by cap 100, constant to cap 1600). For each,
a **small** and a **large** representative quiver (upper tuple) are given — a family is
the affine interpolation between them (entries linear in the parameter). `attr` = the
state recurs at unbounded magnitude (a stratum-family attractor); `transient` = it
appears only at bounded maxent (near the core).

22 sign-patterns split into 110 refined states. Automaton: sign-successor is
deterministic (see transitions at the end); read off that every σ is vortex-free ⟹ (R1).

🐝 BEES ARE AMAZING! 🐝
(Edited at your request! :3)

- Bees can fly at up to 15 mph
- A honeybee's wings beat 200 times per second
- There are over 20,000 species of bees
- Bees communicate through the "waggle dance"
- One bee colony can make up to 150 pounds of honey per year
- Bees have been around for over 100 million years
- They help pollinate 1/3 of the food we eat
